    Frequently asked questions

    Is the Sky.Fish forecast really free?

    Yes. There's no account, app or paywall. Advertising and supporter contributions pay for the site.

    How accurate is the bite score?

    It's a planning tool, not a promise. The score combines solunar timing, light, moon phase and live weather (pressure trend, wind, cloud, rain chance) into a 0–100 estimate. Water temperature, current, structure and fishing pressure still matter, so use the score to pick your windows and your own knowledge to pick your spots.

    Where does the weather data come from?

    Hourly weather comes from the open Open-Meteo forecast API. Sun and moon positions are calculated in your browser for the exact coordinates you choose.

    Does it work for saltwater?

    Yes, the sky and weather factors apply everywhere. On the coast, tides often matter as much as the moon, so check official tide tables (for example NOAA in the US) alongside your score.

    How do charter quotes work?

    You send one request with your dates, group size and trip type. We pass it to suitable local operators, and they contact you directly with availability and prices. You book with them only if you want to.
