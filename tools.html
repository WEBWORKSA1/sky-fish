Angler Tools

Quick calculators for the dock, the boat and the tackle bench. They all run in your browser and work offline once loaded.

Calculator

Fish weight estimator

Measure length (nose to tail fork or tip) and girth (around the fattest part), then estimate weight before you release the fish.

—

Formula: length × girth² ÷ factor (in inches → lb). Estimates are typically within about 10–15%. Use certified scales for records.

Calculator

Reel line capacity

Your reel is rated for one line (e.g. "150 yd of 0.30 mm"). Work out how much of a different line it will hold.

—

Capacity scales with the inverse square of diameter. Check the package for the true diameter, because "lb test" alone varies by brand.
