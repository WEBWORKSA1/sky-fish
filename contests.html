Catch of the Month

Free to enter. Four categories, a winner in each every month, and Angler of the Year at the end of the season.

This month closes in —

🏆

Biggest Catch

Longest fish of the month for its species group, measured on a board or tape and shown in the photo.

📸

Best Photo

Judged on light, composition and story. It doesn't have to be the biggest fish.

🧒

Young Angler

For anglers under 16. Must be entered by a parent or legal guardian.

🌿

Release Hero

Best catch-and-release photo, with the fish handled well and kept wet.

Prizes

What you can win

  • Monthly category winners: a tackle gift card or gear pack. The value is announced at the start of each month with that month's sponsors.
  • Angler of the Year: chosen from the monthly winners, with a headline prize such as a guided trip or premium rod and reel, announced each season.
  • Every winner: featured on Sky.Fish and in The Weekend Bite newsletter.
Brands, shops and charters can sponsor a monthly prize and get credited here.

How judging works

A panel of experienced anglers scores each eligible entry from 1 to 10 on each category's criteria, plus fish handling and ethics. Ties go to the earlier entry. Winners are contacted by email within 10 days of the month closing and must reply within 14 days, or a runner-up is chosen.

Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase or payment necessary. Void where prohibited.
  2. Entrants must be 18+; anglers under 16 enter the Young Angler category through a parent or legal guardian, who is the entrant.
  3. Fish must be caught legally, in season and in line with local size and bag limits during the contest month.
  4. The photo must be your own original image, at least 1080 px on the long edge, with no watermarks. Light edits are fine; manipulated images are disqualified.
  5. Up to 3 entries per person per month (supporters get one bonus entry).
  6. By entering you grant Sky.Fish a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your photo, first name and location on the site, newsletter and social channels, with credit. You keep your copyright.
  7. Entries close at 11:59 pm (your local time) on the last day of the month.
  8. Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Sky.Fish may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.

Full terms are in our terms of use. Contest operated by Sky.Fish.
