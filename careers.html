Turn your time on the water into work

We're building the most useful free fishing resource online. That takes people who actually fish. All roles are remote and flexible.

Open roles

Who we're hiring

Paid per piece, per project or on commission. Tell us what you'd like to do.

Freelance · Paid per article

Fishing Writer

In-depth, first-hand guides on species, techniques and destinations. You'll need clear writing, real experience and respect for accuracy.

Freelance · Regional

Regional Fishing Reporter

Short weekly or bi-weekly reports for your home waters: what's biting, where and on what.

Project-based

Video Creator / Editor

Knot tutorials, technique breakdowns and short-form clips. Underwater or drone footage is a big plus.

Commission

Charter Partnerships Lead

Bring licensed guides and charter operators onto the platform in your region. Base plus commission on featured placements.

Part-time · Remote

Community & Contest Moderator

Review Catch of the Month entries, check rules and handling standards, and help run the judging panel.

Ambassador

Pro Staff / Brand Ambassador

Guides, tournament anglers and creators who represent Sky.Fish at events, on social media and with local clubs.

What we look for

  • Real, recent time on the water. We can tell.
  • Accuracy: you check regulations and facts, and you never invent them.
  • Ethical angling and good fish handling.
  • Reliability: hitting deadlines and communicating clearly.

How it works

  1. Apply with a short note and links to your work.
  2. We reply within a week if there's a fit.
  3. Paid trial assignment.
  4. Ongoing work at agreed rates.

Sky.Fish is an equal-opportunity collaborator. We welcome anglers of every background, gender, age and ability.
