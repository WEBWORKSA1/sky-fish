How it works

Charter & guide FAQ

Is it free to request charter quotes?

Yes. Requesting quotes is free, there's no booking fee, and you don't have to book. You deal with the operator directly.

How much does a fishing charter cost?

Prices vary a lot by region, season, boat size and trip length. As a rough guide, half-day inshore or lake trips are often priced in the hundreds per boat, while full-day offshore trips can run well over a thousand. These are ballpark figures, not quotes. Your replies will show real local prices. Read how to book a charter for what affects price and what to ask.

What's usually included?

Most charters supply rods, reels, tackle and bait, and many clean your catch. Food, drinks, licences, fuel surcharges and the crew tip vary. Ask each operator to list exactly what's included.

Do I need a fishing licence on a charter?

It depends on where you fish. In some places the boat's licence covers passengers; in others each angler needs their own. Check our licence & regulations hub and confirm with the captain.

Is it OK for kids and beginners?

Absolutely. Say so in your request. Many captains specialise in family trips and choose calmer water and faster action.

What if the weather is bad?

Reputable operators reschedule or refund trips cancelled for unsafe conditions. Policies differ, so confirm the weather and cancellation terms before you pay a deposit.
