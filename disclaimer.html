Forecast accuracy

The Sky.Fish bite score is a model that combines astronomical calculations (sun and moon positions, solunar periods, moon phase) with third-party weather forecasts. Fish behaviour depends on many things the model can't see, such as water temperature, clarity, current, tides, fishing pressure and local conditions. Treat forecasts as planning aids, not guarantees.

Safety

Fishing, boating, wading and ice fishing carry real risks. Wear a life jacket, check official marine, weather, river and ice advisories, tell someone your plans and never go out in conditions beyond your experience or equipment. Nothing on this Site is professional safety, medical or legal advice.

Regulations

Licence requirements, seasons, size limits and bag limits vary by location and change often. Our guides give general information only. Always check the current rules with the official agency for the water you fish. See our licence & regulations hub.

Third-party operators

Charter captains and guides who respond to quote requests are independent businesses. Sky.Fish does not employ, supervise or guarantee them. Confirm their credentials, insurance and terms yourself.
