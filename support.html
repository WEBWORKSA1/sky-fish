Where it goes

Transparent by default

We split supporter money across four areas and will report on it in the newsletter.

⚙️ Operations — 40%

Hosting, weather data, security, development of new tools such as tide integration and bite alerts.

👩‍💻 Hiring talent — 25%

Paying freelance writers, regional reporters, guides and video creators for original, expert content.

🏆 Contests & prizes — 20%

Monthly Catch of the Month prizes and the Angler of the Year award.

📣 Promotion — 15%

Getting Sky.Fish in front of new anglers: clubs, youth programmes, events and online marketing.
