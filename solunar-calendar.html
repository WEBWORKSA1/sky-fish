Solunar Fishing Calendar

Plan your month. Each day shows the moon phase, a 1–4 fish rating and the major feeding times for your chosen location.

    —

    Rating dots: 4 = excellent dayTimes shown = major periods (moon overhead / underfoot)Calendar ratings use sky factors; the 7-day forecast adds live weather.

    Using a solunar calendar

    Solunar tables, developed by John Alden Knight in the 1920s and published from the 1930s, predict feeding activity from the position of the moon and sun. The best-rated days usually fall within a few days of the new and full moon, when solar and lunar pull line up. Use the calendar to choose which days to fish, then open the hourly forecast to choose which hours.

    • 4 dots: top days, usually near new or full moon with major periods at dawn or dusk.
    • 3 dots: good days, worth booking a trip around.
    • 1–2 dots: average days. Weather and location matter more than the moon.

    On tidal water, combine this with your local tide table. Moving water around a major period is often the best bite of the month. Want a captain to plan it for you? Request free charter quotes.
