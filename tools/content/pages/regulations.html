Everywhere else

How to find the rules

  1. Search "[your state/province] fishing regulations" and use the government (.gov or equivalent) result.
  2. Ask the local tackle shop or marina.
  3. On a charter, ask the captain whether the boat's licence covers you.

Licence checklist before every trip

  • Valid licence for the right water type (fresh or salt) and any stamps or tags needed
  • Season open for your target species
  • Minimum, maximum or slot size limits and daily bag limits
  • Gear restrictions (barbless hooks, number of rods, live-bait rules)
  • Area closures, sanctuaries and emergency orders
