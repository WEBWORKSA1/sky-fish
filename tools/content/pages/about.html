Anglers have always watched the sky: the moon on the water, a falling barometer, a dark front coming in. Sky.Fish puts those signals into one clear, free forecast, and adds the knowledge and local contacts you need to make the most of it.

What we do

  • Forecasts: an hourly bite score for any location, built from solunar periods, sunrise and sunset, moon phase and live weather.
  • Learning: in-depth guides, a species library, a knot library and curated video lessons.
  • Connections: a simple way to request quotes from local fishing guides and charter captains.
  • Community: a monthly photo contest, a newsletter and paid work for anglers who write, film and guide.

How we make content

Our guides are written and reviewed by the Sky.Fish editorial team using hands-on experience and reputable sources such as fisheries agencies. We don't publish invented statistics or fake reviews. Regulations change, so we always point you to official sources for licences, seasons and limits. Corrections are welcome through our contact page.

How Sky.Fish is funded

The site is free. It's funded by display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, optional featured listings for charter operators and contributions from supporters. Advertisers never decide what we say.
