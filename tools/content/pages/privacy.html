This policy explains what information Sky.Fish ("we", "us") collects when you use sky.fish (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you use a form (charter quote request, operator listing, contest entry, pledge, job application, newsletter or contact form), we receive what you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, trip details and links you provide.
  • Location for forecasts: if you search for a place or tap "Use my location", the coordinates are used in your browser to calculate sun and moon times and are sent to our weather data provider (Open-Meteo) to fetch the forecast. We do not store your precise location on our servers. Your last chosen location is saved in your own browser's local storage so the forecast remembers it.
  • Automatically collected data: like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed and referring pages.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your messages and provide the services you request.
  • To pass your charter or guide quote request to suitable local operators when you ask us to and consent on the form. Those operators then contact you directly and handle your information under their own policies.
  • To run contests, including contacting winners and displaying winning entries as described in the contest rules.
  • To send the newsletter if you subscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the Site secure, prevent spam and improve our content and tools.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). That service processes the data you submit only to deliver it to us. Please don't send sensitive information such as payment card numbers through our forms. Payments are only ever made on a secure hosted checkout page.

4. Cookies and advertising

We use a small amount of local browser storage to remember your forecast location and your cookie choice.

We use Google AdSense to show advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: How Google uses data.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may be shown a consent message, provided through Google's certified consent management tools, before personalised ads are used.

Embedded videos: videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you click play. YouTube's own privacy policy then applies.

5. Third-party services

The Site uses: Google AdSense (advertising), Google Fonts (typography), Open-Meteo (weather and geocoding), YouTube (videos), FormSubmit (form delivery) and GitHub Pages (hosting). Each processes limited data under its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Canada's PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". We will respond within the time required by law.

7. Retention & security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them for, or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect them. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

8. Children

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Young Angler contest entries must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through our contact page or by email.
